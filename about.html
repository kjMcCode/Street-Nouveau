About Us

Street Nouveau is a streetwear brand inspired by a blend of the California surf skate aesthetic and the hip-hop culture of the 1970s through the 1990s. We are defined by the creation and curation of high quality clothing and apparel. We breathe life into our garments with new styles and silhouettes for our customers, ranging from young fashion connoisseurs to those just trying to figure out what works for them. Our goal of reconciling with the divide between affordability and contemporary streetwear will always keep our customers—and their finances—at the center of our business model. Street Nouveau's client focused approach ensures smooth communication, active listening for improvement, and customer satisfaction/retention.

Our Mission

At Street Nouveau, our mission is to encourage individuals to Be The Art, by creating and prioritizing affordable modern streetwear and high-quality fashion. By fostering individuality through the emphasis of style, we want to invigorate a group of people who not only see the art through the clothes they wear, but to embody and personalize their own style.
